# GameBlock — licensed operator models

GameBlock's team fighter models come from Quaternius, **Ultimate Modular Men Pack**:
https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html

Licence: **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original SWAT and Hoodie Character GLBs are archived in this repository
as `public/models/ct_swat.glb` and `public/models/t_hoodie.glb` respectively.

- **Counter-terrorists (CT):** SWAT (Quaternius), source listing
  https://poly.pizza/m/Btfn3G5Xv4
- **Terrorists (T):** Hoodie Character (Quaternius), source listing
  https://poly.pizza/m/gKLBoRsyKe
- Mirror containing the original unmodified Quaternius pack's GLB exports:
  https://github.com/Hhk187/Zomopocalypse/tree/main/Assets/Models/Characters/Ultimate%20Modular%20Men%20Pack-glb

These assets include the humanoid skeleton and 24 named animation clips
(e.g., Idle_Gun, Run_Shoot, Gun_Shoot, Death) each. Source GLB files were
verified through their glTF headers/JSON and skin/animation metadata.

The game, title and characters are **not** official Counter-Strike or Valve
assets. The models are intended to capture a modern tactical/team-shooter
look without reuploading proprietary CS1.6, CSS, CS:GO, or CS2 characters.
